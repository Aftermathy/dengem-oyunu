Frequently asked questions

My game progress disappeared

Your progress is saved to an anonymous device ID and, if you signed in with Apple, to your Apple account. Sign in with the same Apple ID on your device to restore your progress. If it's still missing, email us with your nickname.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app and use the “Restore Purchases” option in the store/settings screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so make sure you're signed in with the account used to buy them.

The leaderboard isn't updating

The leaderboard needs an internet connection. Make sure you're online, then reopen the app. Scores may take a few seconds to sync.

How do I delete my data?

You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing us at the address above. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

We'd love your feedback. Email us with a short description and, if possible, your device model and iOS version.
